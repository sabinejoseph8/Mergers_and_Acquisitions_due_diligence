  4. Restoration (X-09, X-10). The Holbrook report. The lease documents for Puyallup Junction, Kent Valley, Stockton North and Lodi: remaining rent, early termination and surrender terms, and restoration clauses. The accounting memo behind the "indeterminate settlement" conclusion. The auditor's view.

  7. I-5 suit (X-14). The excess insurers' coverage position and any reservation of rights. The insurance tower. Whether punitive damages are pleaded or planned. Claims from other vehicles. Why the $150.0m demand is not in the 10-Q.

  9. Harvest Peak and most-favored-customer clauses (X-12). The Harvest Peak contract, including relocation and assignment terms. Who the two customers requesting reviews are. The Central Valley share of the $169.0m of revenue under most-favored-customer clauses. Whether the clauses would capture Trelliswood's rates after the merger.

  13. FreshArc (X-13). The date of counsel's estimate. The accrual memo. FreshArc's revenue and EBITDA in the LTM. How the $3.9m of unpaid services was treated.
  14. ColdTrack (X-15). The June 30, 2026 impairment memo, the remaining integrator commitments, and the identity of the customers that moved volume.
  15. Capex (X-16). The Holbrook priority list by site. Whether Sandhaven 2 is one of the seven ammonia facilities. The capex classification policy. What the $48.0m of H1 2026 growth capex was for (FIN-19).
  16. Credit agreement (FIN-22). Covenant definitions, levels and headroom, and the change-of-control default.
  17. Change-of-control and assignment clauses (SEC-21). In the leases, the Ironbridge master lease, the Sandhaven concession, and the customer and power contracts.
  18. Ironbridge sale-leaseback (SEC-18). The auditor consultation memo on sale treatment.
