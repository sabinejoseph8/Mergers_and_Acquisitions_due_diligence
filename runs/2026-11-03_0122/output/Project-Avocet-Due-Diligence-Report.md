## 1. Executive summary

### Bottom line

**Proposed recommendation: Do not proceed at the current price of $34.00 per share.** The Investment Committee makes the decision.

The deal is priced at 11.0x LTM Adjusted EBITDA of $250.0m. The verified findings show that this base is too high. On the project's normalization rule, the base is $217.5m to $222.5m, and the operating trend is still falling (X-01, X-02). About $31.0m of the base ends with a port concession in September 2028 (X-05). Lease-end restoration obligations of $312.0m are not on the balance sheet, and Trelliswood's own integration plan triggers $118.0m of them (X-09). The overlap in Puget Sound and the Northern Central Valley could block closing within the proposed $15.0m divestiture cap (X-07).

### Top five red flags

1. **Merger antitrust review could block closing (X-07, Critical).** Combined capacity shares are about 43% in Puget Sound and 39% in the Northern Central Valley. At its June 2026 investor day, Trelliswood said it consolidates acquired capacity and "hand[s] back the older leased boxes". The deal plan does exactly this in both markets. The divestiture cap covers about 6% of Brindlewick's EBITDA, against 41.8% of its capacity in the two markets.
2. **The earnings base is overstated (X-01, Critical).** Normalized LTM is $217.5m to $222.5m. At 11.0x, value is $302.5m to $357.5m (11.0% to 13.0% of EV) below the deal EV, or $26.55 to $27.70 per share (projection).
3. **Operating trend is falling (X-02, Critical).** Revenue growth went from +6.8% to -2.3%, occupancy fell to 81.4%, and FY2026 guidance was cut 11.8%. The annualized H1 2026 fall of $42.0m is $462.0m (16.8% of EV) at 11.0x. This is the Critical test number, not a proposed price cut. The gap to the deal base is $154.0m to $231.0m (projection).
4. **Sandhaven earnings end September 30, 2028 (X-05, Critical).** Sandhaven 1 and 2 earn $31.0m (12.4%) of LTM Adjusted EBITDA. The Harbor District's executive director says there will be no extension. That is $341.0m (12.4% of EV) at 11.0x. The deal brief does not mention it.
5. **Unrecorded lease-end restoration of $312.0m (X-09, Critical).** This is 11.3% of EV and 48.6% of reported equity. Surrendering the four leases in the integration plan triggers $118.0m, 2.6 times the $45.0m integration budget.

Also Critical: the **antitrust class action** (X-08). Treble damages with joint and several liability give an upper bound of $1,800.0m (65.5% of EV). Brindlewick's own 8% share, trebled, is $144.0m (5.2%). Nothing is accrued.

### Main positive and mitigating points

- **Debt maturities:** none before March 2030, and all debt can be repaid at par, so repaying it at closing costs no premium (SEC-23).
- **Customer retention and concentration:** about 1,310 customers, the largest at 5.7% of revenue and the top ten at 34.3% (FIN-27, SEC-20). *Qualified:* the tenth-largest customer, FreshArc, has liquidated (X-13), active customers fell 1.1% in H1 2026 (FIN-30), and two customers moved volume after ColdTrack failures (SEC-06).
- **Segment still growing:** value-added services grew 17.4% in FY2025 and 13.2% in H1 2026 (SEC-45).
- **Items correctly excluded from adjusted metrics:** the $46.0m 2024 sale-leaseback gain is kept out of Adjusted EBITDA and is outside the LTM (SEC-03).
- **Dismissed, settled or closed litigation:** the Greenfield Produce claim ($22.5m) was dismissed with prejudice, with no appeal (LIT-07). The Westmark claim ($33.0m) is covered by an uncapped indemnity that Arcturus accepted in writing without reservation of rights (LIT-06).
- **Supply or price protections:** the $0.50 per pound warehouse-receipt cap limits the Coastline claim to about $1.24m, pending a December 11, 2026 ruling (LIT-05). *Qualified:* fixed-price power runs only to December 31, 2026, before closing (X-03).
- **Internal controls and auditor:** controls were judged effective at December 31, 2025 and June 30, 2026. Audit opinions were unqualified, and Pemberton & Hale has audited the company since 2009 (SEC-36). *Qualified:* three practices in the same periods are called into question (X-10).
- **Cash conversion:** operating cash flow has been a steady 67% to 75% of Adjusted EBITDA (FIN-31).

## 2. Answers to the six Investment Committee questions

### Q1. Earnings base: is $250.0m of LTM Adjusted EBITDA a sound base for 11.0x?

No. The $250.0m recomputes correctly (FIN-01), but its quality does not support 11.0x.

- **Normalized LTM is $217.5m to $222.5m (X-01, projection).** The bridge from $250.0m:
  - less $3.7m of benefit from the insurance accrual cut (X-04);
  - plus $4.6m of ColdTrack credits, if they do not recur (SEC-06);
  - less $17.7m a year for repricing the 2021 fixed-price power contracts from January 1, 2027 (X-03);
  - less $4.7m a year of property tax that the change of control triggers (SEC-16);
  - less the FreshArc preference loss, $6.0m to $11.0m, which counsel calls likely and the company has not accrued (X-13).
- **The trend is still down (X-02).** H1 2026 Adjusted EBITDA fell 15.1%. The CFO said the second half would not look "very different from the first". The H1 run-rate of $236.0m is $14.0m below the deal base. On run-rate, forward normalized earnings are $220.1m before property tax and FreshArc.
- **Sandhaven (X-05).** $31.0m of the base ends September 30, 2028.
- **Growth link.** The two facilities that drove FY2025 growth, Calder Valley and Bayfront, carry $61.5m of the unrecorded restoration (X-09). The deal multiple is applied to earnings that include them (inference).
- **Smaller items:**
  - severance and transaction costs labeled non-recurring come back each year (SEC-02);
  - the 2025 additions flatter H1 2026 against H1 2025 (FIN-11).

### Q2. Unrecorded obligations: what sits outside net debt or off the balance sheet?

Net debt of $1,118.0m ties to the balance sheet (FIN-21). The following sit outside it:

- **Lease-end restoration (X-09, Critical):** $312.0m, unrecorded and undiscounted. Our plan triggers $118.0m of it at Puyallup Junction, Kent Valley, Stockton North and Lodi. The integration budget does not include it (FIN-25). Restatement risk (inference): FY2025, its FY2024 comparative and June 30, 2026.
- **Antitrust class action (X-08, Critical):** no accrual. The upper bound is $1,800.0m; Brindlewick's own share, trebled, is $144.0m.
- **I-5 fatal crash (X-14, High):** a $150.0m demand against $55.0m of combined retention and excess cover. Only the $5.0m retention is reserved.
- **FreshArc preference loss (X-13, Medium):** $6.0m to $11.0m, which counsel calls likely, is not accrued.
- **Other possible debt-like items (FIN-26):**
  - accrued claims reserves of $44.1m (adequacy in doubt, X-04);
  - other non-current liabilities of $25.5m;
  - change-in-control severance of about $16.4m, not accrued.
- **Deferred maintenance (X-16):** $96.0m of priority replacements in 2027 to 2029.
- **Sale-leaseback accounting (SEC-18):** sale treatment of the $214.0m Ironbridge transaction rests on a judgment. If it failed, the $214.0m would become a financing obligation (inference).
- **Not found:** the 10-K reports no pension or multiemployer plan, and no employee is represented by a union (SEC-22). Operating lease liabilities of $668.0m are outside net debt, consistent with the EBITDA definition (FIN-24).

### Q3. Antitrust: can the combination clear within the $15.0m divestiture cap and the outside date?

On the sources, the deal may not clear on the proposed terms (X-07, Critical, test: could block closing).

- **Combined shares** are about 43% in Puget Sound and 39% in the Northern Central Valley. That raises HHI by 912 and 756 points (arithmetic). The shares come from different data sets, so they are approximate.
- **Trelliswood's public statements** describe buying and closing acquired capacity in overlap markets. The integration plan closes four Brindlewick sites in those two markets: 234k pallets, 20.4% of Brindlewick's capacity.
- **The cap is small against the overlap:**
  - $15.0m is 6.0% of LTM Adjusted EBITDA, against 41.8% of Brindlewick's capacity in the two markets;
  - Sandhaven alone earns $31.0m, 2.1 times the cap.
  - If a reviewer asks for more, Trelliswood has no obligation to divest more. The deal could then fail, and the $110.0m reverse termination fee would be payable.
- **Class action link:** the class action (X-08) alleges that operators exchanged rates "by market" through Coldmetric. Trelliswood cites Coldmetric data for its own shares. Whether Trelliswood subscribed to Coldmetric, or is one of the six unnamed defendants, is not in the sources.
- **Timing:** the class certification motion is due February 26, 2027, during the expected review.
- **Possible arguments for the defense (inference):**
  - about 140,000 new speculative positions in the Central Valley (NEWS-02);
  - customers can build their own;
  - Sandhaven's exit in 2028 would cut Brindlewick's Puget Sound capacity by 38.8%.
- **Gaps:** no source gives a reviewer's position or facility-level EBITDA, so antitrust counsel must assess the remedy and the timetable.

### Q4. Cost of the deal: what will it cost beyond the equity price, and how should it be presented?

- **Known deal costs (X-11, High):**
  - equity award cash-out: $46.2m;
  - change-in-control severance: $16.4m;
  - reverse termination fee: $110.0m;
  - together, $172.6m (6.3% of EV) under the project rule, which adds the three categories.
  - The reverse fee is paid only if the deal fails on antitrust grounds. If the deal closes, the cost is $62.6m (2.3%).
- **Fees:** advisory, financing and other fees of $72.0m are a use of funds, not part of the test.
- **Presentation:**
  - Fully diluted EV is $2,796.2m (11.18x LTM). The all-in cost including severance is $2,812.6m (11.25x), not 11.0x (SEC-38).
  - The sources and uses and the integration budget both leave out the $16.4m severance (SEC-38).
  - The severance plan was adopted September 21, 2026, the day before the strategic review was announced, and it raised the multiples (SEC-40).
  - PSUs vest at target under the plan, so the $46.2m recomputes (SEC-39).
- **Other cash costs the deal itself triggers** (not in the test, but in the price discussion):
  - $118.0m of restoration (X-09);
  - the $45.0m integration budget;
  - $4.7m a year of property tax (SEC-16).

### Q5. Contracts and facilities: what changes after 2026 or on a change of control?

- **Power (X-03, High):** the 2021 fixed-price contracts covering 70% of use at $61/MWh end December 31, 2026. At 2027 to 2029 offers of about $104/MWh, cost rises $17.7m a year, worth $195.0m (7.1% of EV) (projection). No pass-through to customers is shown.
- **Sandhaven concession (X-05, Critical):** expires September 30, 2028, with no renewal option. The improvements revert to the District.
- **Harvest Peak and most-favored-customer clauses (X-12, High):**
  - a seven-year contract signed July 2026 at about 9% below the rate card;
  - two existing customers have already asked for most-favored-customer reviews;
  - upper bound $15.2m a year on $169.0m of revenue under those clauses (projection).
  - Two of the three Harvest Peak sites, Stockton North and Lodi, are among the four leases we plan to surrender (NEWS-09).
- **Four surrendered leases:** $118.0m of restoration (X-09). Remaining rent and termination rights are not in the sources (SEC-21).
- **Change of control:**
  - California property tax reassessment adds $4.7m a year (SEC-16);
  - the severance plan pays $16.4m (X-11);
  - equity awards that are not assumed vest in full (SEC-39);
  - all debt can be repaid at par (SEC-23).
  - Change-of-control and assignment terms of the leases, the Ironbridge master lease, the concession, and the customer and power contracts are not disclosed (SEC-21).
- **ColdTrack (X-15):** halting the rollout, as the plan proposes, strands $47.0m of capitalized software (inference, non-cash).

### Limitations

- **Fictional data.** This is a fictional case study. Every company, person and figure is invented.
- **Excerpts only.** The SEC files are excerpts, and the litigation files are summaries, not full dockets. Where a fact is "not found", it may sit in parts of the documents that were not provided.
- **Labeled conclusions.** Projections, normalizations and inferences are labeled. They depend on the stated assumptions, in particular the 2027 power price, whether the ColdTrack credits recur, and the size of the FreshArc loss.
- **Market shares.** They come from two different data sets, so the combined shares are approximate.
- **Missing data.** No facility-level EBITDA, lease documents, insurance contracts, actuarial reports or antitrust analysis was available.
- **Not advice.** The report is a draft for human decision-makers and is not investment, legal or accounting advice.
