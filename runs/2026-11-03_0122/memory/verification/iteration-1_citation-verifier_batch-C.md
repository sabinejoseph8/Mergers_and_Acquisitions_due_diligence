**Most important corrections**

1. **FIN-25 (lease restoration).** The 10-K states the cost the card could not find. Restoration at the four surrendered sites is $118.0m, and across all eleven leases $312.0m is unrecognized. That is 11.3% of EV, which meets the Critical test under the off-balance-sheet rule. The $118.0m (4.3%) is the near-term cash cost the plan triggers. FIN-25 should be at least High.
2. **FIN-17 (electricity).** The contracts expire on December 31, 2026, before closing. At about $104/MWh the cost rises about $17.7m a year, or $195.0m at 11.0x (7.1%) (projection). Recommend High.
3. **Known deal costs (FIN-35, FIN-37).** CIC severance is about $16.4m (8-K). The total is $62.6m (2.3%) without the $110.0m reverse termination fee and $172.6m (6.3%) with it. With it, the rolled-up finding is High under the rubric; state that the reverse fee is contingent.
4. **FIN-03 (normalization).** It covers the insurance accrual and severance only, so its wording must say so. Its 11.3% case sits below company guidance. As an indication for the lead's X- bridge (projection): 245.3 - 17.7 (power) - 4.7 (property tax) = 222.9, which is $298.1m (10.8%) below the EV at 11.0x. This is before any add-back of the $4.6m ColdTrack credits.
5. **FIN-02 (earnings fall).** Critical stands under the rubric and on the company's guidance (10.8% to 14.0%). However, $462.0m is not a price cut: the gap to the $250.0m base is $154.0m to $231.0m.
6. **FIN-11 (acquisition timing).** Bayfront was acquired in March 2025. Calder Valley was a new build opened in June 2025. Drop the "about one month" inference.
7. **FIN-26 (debt-like items).** Drop the multiemployer point (the 10-K says no employee is union-represented). Cite the $96m (FIN-18) and the $312.0m (FIN-25) by ID.
8. **FIN-28 (FreshArc).** FreshArc ceased operations in March 2026. Raise to Medium.
9. **FIN-20 (ColdTrack).** The software balance is "principally" ColdTrack, not all of it. The 10-Q's June 30, 2026 no-impairment conclusion needs an X- dated-judgment test.
10. **FIN-04 (insurance accrual).** Replace the card's speculative explanation with the 10-Q's stated cause (telematics), which the +18.3% H1-over-H1 accident rate contradicts.
