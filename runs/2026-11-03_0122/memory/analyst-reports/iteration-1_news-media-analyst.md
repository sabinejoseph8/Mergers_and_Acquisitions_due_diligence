#### NEWS-01: 2026 guidance cut; H1 2026 Adjusted EBITDA down 15.1%
- **Finding:** On August 6, 2026, Brindlewick cut FY2026 Adjusted EBITDA guidance to $236m to $244m, from $268m to $276m in February. The midpoint fell $32.0m, or 11.8%. H1 Adjusted EBITDA fell 15.1% to $118.0m from $139.0m, and the CFO said H2 would look much like H1. The deal's $250.0m LTM includes H2 2025 at $132.0m ($271.0m less $139.0m). Revised guidance implies H2 2026 of $118.0m to $126.0m (midpoint $122.0m).
- **Evidence:** "now expects 2026 Adjusted EBITDA of $236 million to $244 million, down from $268 million to $276 million in February." CFO: "We don't see the second half looking very different from the first."
- **Source:** `targets/avocet/data/news/2026-08-07_capital-markets-wire_q2-results-guidance-cut.md` (Capital Markets Wire, Aug 7, 2026), para. 1, 2, 4 (lines 7, 9, 13); `targets/avocet/data/news/2026-02-26_capital-markets-wire_brindlewick-q4-results.md` (Feb 26, 2026), para. 1, 3 (lines 7, 11).
- **Claim type:** Fact.
- **Severity:** High. All figures below are projections.
  - The guidance midpoint of $240.0m is $10.0m below the LTM. At 11.0x that is $110.0m, or 4.0% of EV.
  - The low end of $236.0m equals H1 2026 annualized ($118.0m x 2). It is $14.0m below the LTM: $154.0m, or 5.6% of EV.
  - The high end of $244.0m is $6.0m below the LTM: $66.0m, or 2.4% of EV.
  - Taking NEWS-04's $3.7m off the midpoint gives $236.3m. That is $13.7m below the LTM: $150.7m, or 5.5% of EV.
  - No case reaches the $25.0m EBITDA gap needed for 10% of EV, so this is not Critical.
- **Confidence:** High. These are company figures. The deal brief's LTM arithmetic ($271.0m less $139.0m plus $118.0m = $250.0m) matches them.
- **Deal impact:** Valuation (IC Q1 earnings base).
- **Cross-check:** Q2 2026 10-Q and earnings 8-K for guidance and H1 figures. Financial data for quarterly Adjusted EBITDA and its reconciliation. Q3 2026 results (not found in news coverage) to test whether H2 is tracking $118.0m to $126.0m.

#### NEWS-12: Antitrust: Trelliswood's stated shares in both overlap markets

Brindlewick competes in both markets. Brindlewick's share was not found in news coverage, so the combined share cannot be computed. The deal's limits are:
  - divestitures capped at $15.0m of LTM Adjusted EBITDA (6.0% of Brindlewick's $250.0m);
