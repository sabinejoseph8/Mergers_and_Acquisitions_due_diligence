### Findings

#### NEWS-01: Halsey Rock's appraisal demand alone exceeds the buyer's 10% walk-away right
- **Finding:** Meridale's largest holder (11.4%) says it will vote against the deal and demand appraisal of all its shares. The merger agreement reportedly lets the buyer walk away if holders of more than 10% seek appraisal. Halsey Rock alone is over that threshold.
- **Evidence:** "will vote against the US$41.50-a-share sale ... and, if the deal is approved, demand appraisal of all its shares in Delaware". Also: "lets Ostrander Hale walk away if holders of more than 10% of the shares seek appraisal".
- **Source:** `targets/wren/data/news/2026-10-06_capital-markets-wire_halsey-rock-opposes-meridale-deal.md` (CMW, Oct 6, 2026), paras 1 and 3
- **Claim type:** Fact (Halsey Rock's stated intention, on record in its letter). The walk-right term is as CMW reports it.
- **Severity:** Critical. Test met: block closing. 11.4% is above the 10% dissenting-shares threshold, so the buyer's closing condition fails if Halsey Rock follows through.
- **Confidence:** Medium. The letter is on record, but the 10% term comes from secondary reporting and needs the merger agreement.
- **Deal impact:** Closing risk. Possible appraisal cost above US$41.50 per share.
- **Cross-check:** Merger agreement dissenting-shares condition (exact threshold, and whether the buyer can waive it); proxy beneficial ownership table (Halsey Rock 11.4%); Schedule 13D/13G filings and any 13D amendment attaching the letter.
