### Most important corrections

1. **FIN-03 and FIN-10:** the filings state the amortization benefit: 5.1 in the LTM (1.7 in Q4 2025 plus 3.4 in H1 2026), not the 7.3 proxy. The corrected normalized base is 157.3 to 161.4. That implies 2,265.1 to 2,324.2 at 14.4x, which is 5.5% to 7.9% below deal EV (projection).
2. **FIN-29:** known deal costs are 169.6 (6.9% of EV), not 116.3 (4.7%). The missing items are severance 16.8, the Brockmere termination fee 9.5 and the Pellham Rowe fee contingent on closing 27.0. Recommend High.
3. **FIN-32:** the 10-K says a partial withdrawal would trigger a proportional liability. The complete withdrawal estimate is 338. Pro rata for the 14 branches this is about 99.7 (inference), 30.1% of reported equity. Recommend High.
4. **FIN-30 and FIN-31:** the 10-K confirms an existing 11.2 a year Section 382 limit that a new ownership change cannot raise. The model's 65.2 limit does not apply. Usable in FY2027 to FY2029 is about 7.1 tax-effected, vs 66.8 in the model. Make FIN-31 the parent and raise its confidence to High.
5. **FIN-08:** the out-of-period charge relates to FY2025 (unaccrued Southwest subcontractor costs), so audited FY2025 is the period at risk. The lead should record the X- finding against the FY2025 effective-ICFR conclusion.
6. **FIN-26:** the 101% put and the absence of repurchase financing are confirmed by the 10-K Note 9 and the DEFM14A. Add those citations. The notes trade at 97.25%. High stays under the calibration rule.
7. **FIN-25:** pro forma interest cover may be nearer 1.6x than 1.9x once the 1.92% SOFR swap is stripped out.
8. **FIN-16 and FIN-14:** the card's statements that "not quantified" or "not disclosed" are wrong. Pinecrest revenue was 51.0 (growth excluding Pinecrest 3.3%; H1 2026 -3.1%), and the LTE-M replacement cost is about 5.2.
