1. **Adjusted EBITDA questions are answered by the financial data (SEC-02, SEC-07, SEC-59, SEC-61).** In `financials/cash_flow.csv`, the D&A line (42.8) equals the add-back, and contract-cost amortization sits in the working-capital line. So the US$5.1m benefit from the amortization change is inside LTM Adjusted EBITDA (inference, strong). The H1 2026 reconciliation shows the US$4.1m and US$5.2m charges were not added back. As a result, SEC-61's H1 comparison should be 16.4%, not 14.6%.
2. **Code compliance (SEC-53 parent, SEC-13 supporting).** Critical is acceptable only as a labeled upper bound. The per-unit cost must cite the Vertical Transport Journal articles, since it is not in any filing. The range assumes all 22,400 units are affected, but the source says "most". Counsel's view came after the 10-Q, so it puts the 10-Q position in doubt rather than conflicting with it on the same date. Keep High only with a named special indemnity or escrow. St. Aldric is the 6th-largest customer (US$14.8m in FY2025).
