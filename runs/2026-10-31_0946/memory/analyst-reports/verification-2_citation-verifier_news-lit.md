Corrected numbers: the gap in Ridgeline's favor is US$15.5m, the break-even price is US$26.91 (equity value US$1,399.3m, 73.3 above the bid), and the fee depends on a Ridgeline closing ("effective at closing", per the 8-K).
3. **NEWS-002 is out of date.** The 8-K of July 22, 2026 extended the Borealis agreement to Dec 31, 2029. Concentration is confirmed (34.8% in FY2025, 39.3% in H1 2026), but the "renewal near closing" reason for High no longer holds. Recommend Medium or a supporting ID.
4. **NEWS-001 overstates its source and is superseded.** The source says "plans to" and "could reduce". Quillmark's actual 8-K terms (prices cut 6.0%, co-op 6.0% to 10.5%) cost about US$23.3m a year at FY2025 volume before any offsets (inference), 16.5% of LTM Adjusted EBITDA. The parent 8-K finding should be tested for Critical on these figures.
5. **NEWS-007 should not go to Low.** The 10-K and 10-Q link Quillmark to Meridian (41% of footwear imports, US$2.9m of poorly documented payments, an FCPA self-report). Make SEC-030 the parent.
6. **Company data backs two news items.** kpis.csv shows 15.6 weeks of Borealis supply at Jun 30, 2026 (NEWS-010) and a 31.0% clearance recovery rate in H1 2026 (NEWS-009). Both sit against the Q2 2026 reserve release and the US$3.1m returns reserve. NEWS-010 also needs the "where necessary" qualifier.
7. **SEC-023 needs its proviso.** The licensee "remains bound by all terms", so the assignment clause does not reduce the Section 14.3 key-person exposure (LIT-009/SEC-020, Critical, verified).
8. **NEWS-006: correct the Summitline wording.** Only Borealis's letter is quoted and was reviewed by the outlet; Summitline "issued a similar notice". Borealis (rank 1, US$236.0m) and Summitline (rank 2, US$58.6m) together are 43.5% of FY2025 revenue.

10. **Possible 10-K disclosure gap.** The registry invalidation (Feb 9, 2026, per The Climate Desk) came 17 days before the 10-K was filed (Feb 26, 2026). The 10-K still describes the Rio Verde credits as support for the claim and does not mention the invalidation.
