### Findings

Cards are ordered by severity: Critical, then High, Medium, Low.

#### FIN-001: H1 2026 inventory reserve release drives the record gross margin and all of the H1 Adjusted EBITDA increase
- **Finding:** The excess and obsolete (E&O) inventory reserve fell by 14.7 in H1 2026 (22.6 to 7.9) while aged inventory rose by 28.0. A release of this size through cost of revenue is larger than the whole H1 2026 gross margin expansion and the whole 13.0 rise in H1 Adjusted EBITDA. Without it, the H1 2026 gross margin is 48.5%, not 53.0%, and LTM Adjusted EBITDA is 126.3, not 141.0.
- **Evidence:**
  - Reserve change = 7.9 - 22.6 = -14.7. Aged inventory change = 109.0 - 81.0 = +28.0. Clearance recovery fell from 36% to 31%. None of these suggests the reserved stock was sold or scrapped.
  - Gross inventory = net + reserve: 238.0 + 22.6 = 260.6 (Dec 31, 2025) and 284.0 + 7.9 = 291.9 (Jun 30, 2026). Gross build 31.3 + reserve decrease 14.7 = 46.0, which equals cash flow "Change in inventories" of -46.0. The decrease is therefore non-cash.
  - Cost of revenue fell 154.5 -> 153.2 (-1.3) while revenue rose 306.0 -> 326.0 (+20.0, +6.5%).
  - Gross margin: H1 2025 = 151.5 / 306.0 = 49.5%. H2 2025 = (339.0 - 151.5) / (678.0 - 306.0) = 187.5 / 372.0 = 50.4%. H1 2026 reported = 172.8 / 326.0 = 53.0%. H1 2026 excluding release = (172.8 - 14.7) / 326.0 = 158.1 / 326.0 = 48.5%, the lowest half in the data.
  - Margin expansion in gross profit terms = 172.8 - (326.0 x 49.51%) = 172.8 - 161.4 = 11.4. That is less than the 14.7 release, so the underlying margin fell by about 1.0 point (3.3 / 326.0).
  - H1 Adj. EBITDA excluding release = 67.0 - 14.7 = 52.3, against 54.0 in H1 2025 (-3.1%). LTM = 141.0 - 14.7 = 126.3, against 128.0 in FY2025 (-1.3%).
  - Severity test (more than 10% of value): the bid multiple is 1,561.0 / 141.0 = 11.07x. 14.7 x 11.07 = 162.7 of EV, which is 162.7 / 1,561.0 = 10.4% of EV (and 162.7 / 1,326.0 = 12.3% of equity value).
- **Source:** `targets/plover/data/financials/kpis.csv` lines 14-16 (aged inventory, E&O reserve, recovery rate); `balance_sheet.csv` line 4 (Inventories, net); `income_statement.csv` lines 5-7; `cash_flow.csv` line 6; `adjusted_ebitda_reconciliation.csv` line 9; `README.md` line 20 (net inventory is after the reserve); `deal-brief.md` section 2.
- **Severity:** Critical. Test met: value changes by more than 10% (10.4% of EV at the bid's own multiple).
- **Confidence:** Medium. The reserve movement, the margins and the tie-out are calculated directly from company data. That the 14.7 went through cost of revenue as a release, rather than being used against write-offs, is an inference from the rise in aged inventory, the fall in cost of revenue and the non-cash tie-out. A reserve rollforward would confirm or rule it out.
- **Deal impact:** Valuation. The LTM base of 141.0 is overstated by 14.7, and investment thesis point 3 (record 53.0% margin) does not hold on an underlying basis.
- **Cross-check:** Q1 and Q2 2026 10-Qs: the inventory note (reserve rollforward), the MD&A gross margin bridge, and any disclosed change in reserve estimate or method. The 10-K critical accounting estimates for inventory. Earnings-call coverage in the news explaining the margin. Any auditor or SEC comment-letter correspondence.

#### FIN-005: Borealis Market channel inventory jumped to 15.6 weeks; an estimated 26.2 to 30.4 of H1 2026 revenue is channel fill
- **Finding:** Borealis's weeks of supply of Quillmark product rose from 8.9 at December 31, 2025 to 15.6 at June 30, 2026. Quillmark books revenue when it sells to Borealis (wholesale vendor model), so the build in Borealis's stock is revenue Quillmark has already booked. On my estimate it equals or exceeds the whole 20.0 H1 revenue increase.
- **Evidence:**
  - Weeks of supply: 6.8, 7.4, 8.9, 15.6. The increase is 15.6 - 8.9 = 6.7 weeks (+75%, 15.6/8.9 = 1.75).
  - Method 1, constant weekly sell-through S in H1 2026: sell-in 128.0 = 26S + 6.7S = 32.7S, so S = 3.914. Channel build = 6.7 x 3.914 = 26.2. Sell-through = 26 x 3.914 = 101.8.

  - H1 revenue growth excluding the fill: (326.0 - 26.2)/306.0 = -2.0%; (326.0 - 30.4)/306.0 = -3.4%. (inference)

  - Counter-indicator: receivables rose only 3.0 in H1 2026 (71.0 to 74.0).
  - Caveats: the weeks-of-supply basis is not defined, and June 30, 2025 is not given, so the June-vs-December comparison may include seasonality.
- **Source:** `targets/plover/data/financials/kpis.csv` lines 4, 6, 7; `revenue_by_customer.csv` line 2 (wholesale vendor model); `income_statement.csv` line 5; `balance_sheet.csv` line 3.

- **Confidence:** Medium. The weeks-of-supply figures are company data. The revenue estimate depends on the sell-through assumptions.
- **Deal impact:** Valuation and revenue durability. H2 2026 sell-in risk if Borealis destocks. Possible return, markdown or price-protection obligations.
