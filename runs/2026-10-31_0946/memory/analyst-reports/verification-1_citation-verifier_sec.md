### Most important corrections

1. **SEC-028: raise from High to Critical.** The FTC complaint (targets/plover/data/litigation/LIT-001_ftc-evertrail.md, "Relief sought") asks for refunds equal to EverTrail net revenue from 2023 to 2025. kpis.csv gives 52.4 + 71.8 + 88.3 = US$212.5m, which is 13.6% of EV, as an upper bound. Under the remedy-sought rule this meets the more-than-10% test. Also record the gap: the 10-Q says it "cannot estimate a range" even though the complaint defines how to measure the refund.
2. **SEC-040: wrong timing.** The offer expires one minute after the go-shop ends. The two do not end "at the same time" or "at the same moment".
3. **SEC-021: two overstatements.** The 10-Q does not say that no accrual was recorded. Say "no accrual or range disclosed". Section 1.12 does not name markdown allowances, and it allows trade discounts shown on the invoice.
4. **SEC-025: source gap.** US$295.0m needs the deal brief s.2 and balance_sheet.csv (term loan 240.0). The cited 10-K and 10-Q give 250.0 + 55.0 = 305.0.
5. **SEC-044: label the inference.** "Outside the 52.0m shares" is an inference. The 8-K's "offer price" is Ridgeline's US$24.00 (US$38.4m). US$40.8m assumes the same cash-out at US$25.50.
6. **SEC-016: unsupported comparison.** "About three times as fast as revenue" is not supported. FY2025 is 2.6x, and the H1 comparison mixes a six-month balance change with year-on-year revenue growth.
7. **Wording fixes:** SEC-041 ("in other specified circumstances", not "otherwise"), SEC-045 (11.4 is cash severance and benefits), SEC-046 ("the only non-compete disclosed"), SEC-018 (cite deal brief s.2 for net debt).
8. **Severity flags for the lead's X- work, not errors in the SEC cards:**
   - SEC-007: Borealis weeks of supply rose from 8.9 to 15.6, and Retail Ledger (Jul 30, 2026) says Borealis will use its return rights.
   - SEC-033: the county staff report recommends 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. operations only, with a hearing on Nov 18, 2026 and a second site estimated at about US$45m.
   - Both may warrant High once combined with these sources.
9. **The four Critical findings hold, each with its test shown:**
   - SEC-004: 16.5% of EV (projection).
   - SEC-011: 10.9% of EV at 11.07x. It confirms the 15.4 is not added back, but it is only just over the threshold.
   - SEC-020: 33.0% of EV, as value at risk (upper bound).
   - SEC-039: could stop the deal (71.8% of votes).
