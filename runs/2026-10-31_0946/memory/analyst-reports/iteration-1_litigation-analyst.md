#### LIT-001: FTC seeks refunds equal to three years of EverTrail net revenue, plus a 20-year compliance order
- **Finding:** The FTC formally seeks refunds equal to all of Quillmark's net revenue from EverTrail products for calendar 2023 to 2025. It also seeks a permanent injunction with a 20-year compliance order. That cost can be computed from company data. However, EverTrail revenue is not found in the litigation records or the SEC filing excerpts, so I cannot yet apply the more-than-10%-of-value test. For that reason I rate it High, not Critical.
- **Evidence:**
  - Complaint filed June 18, 2026, prayer for relief: "Monetary relief, including refunds to consumers, in an amount equal to Defendant's net revenue from sales of EverTrail products from January 1, 2023 through December 31, 2025." Also: "A permanent injunction against unsubstantiated environmental claims, with a 20-year compliance order."
  - Detail file: "The complaint does not state a dollar amount for monetary relief."
  - Procedural history: civil investigative demand November 2025; motion to dismiss August 14, 2026 ("claims were substantiated when made"); FTC opposition September 25, 2026; reply October 9, 2026; hearing December 3, 2026.
  - Allegations (untested): FTC lab tests of 14 products found 29% to 46% recycled nylon. A 2023 third-party report sent to Quillmark warned that the Rio Verde project baseline was "materially overstated". The Global Offset Registry invalidated the project's credits in February 2026.
- **Exposure:**
  - **Upper bound:** FY2023 to FY2025 EverTrail net revenue, not yet computed.
  - **Thresholds (projection):** 10% of equity value is US$132.6m (US$1,326.0m x 10%). 10% of enterprise value is US$156.1m. The remedy would pass the equity threshold if EverTrail net revenue averaged more than US$44.2m a year over the three years (US$132.6m / 3).
  - **Not used for the test:** civil penalties "up to the statutory maximum" for future violations are a ceiling, not a remedy sought.
  - **Could be larger:** the motion to dismiss could be denied on December 3, 2026. The alleged 2023 warning report bears directly on the substantiation defense.
  - **Could be smaller:** the case could be dismissed. No source gives a lower measure. The alleged 22% average price premium is a factual allegation, not the remedy sought.
  - No source shows the remedy is unlikely, so it should not be discounted.
- **Non-monetary risk:**
  - The injunction is worded against "unsubstantiated environmental claims" generally, not only EverTrail. A 20-year order would constrain marketing across the portfolio after closing.
  - Reputational harm to a sustainability-positioned line.
  - The 10-K says the "Carbon-Neutral" label was dropped from new production in January 2026. Whether the "100% Recycled Nylon" claim is still in use is not found in litigation records.
- **Trend:** Risk has increased. The matter moved from a civil investigative demand (November 2025) to a filed complaint (June 18, 2026). The court has not ruled. The next turning point is December 3, 2026, after the go-shop ends.
- **Source:** `targets/plover/data/litigation/LIT-001_ftc-evertrail.md`, complaint June 18, 2026 (Relief sought; Procedural history). `docket_index.csv`, row LIT-001.
- **Severity:** High. Raise to Critical, labeled as an upper bound, if FY2023 to FY2025 EverTrail net revenue is above US$132.6m.
- **Confidence:** High that the remedy is formally sought (stated in the court record). Low on the merits (untested allegations).
- **Deal impact:** Valuation (possibly more than 10% of value). Post-closing compliance burden. Disclosure (no accrual; see LIT-008). Not a closing condition under the Ridgeline terms.
- **Cross-check:** Ask the financial analyst for EverTrail net revenue by year for FY2023 to FY2025. Check news for the registry invalidation date and FTC statements. 10-Q Note 9 says no accrual has been recorded.
