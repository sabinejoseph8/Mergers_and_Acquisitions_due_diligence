Open questions and management requests

Conflicts between sources

  1. Size of the reserve release. The 10-Q gives a change in estimate of US$15.4m. The data pack shows a net reserve decrease of US$14.7m (22.6 to 7.9). The 0.7 gap is unexplained. X-001 uses the 10-Q figure. (SEC-011, FIN-001)
  2. Founder's shares. The deal brief and NEWS-018 use 10.4m shares. The 8-K and proxy show 10.4m Class B plus 0.6m Class A, 11.0m in total. X-012 uses 11.0m. (SEC-039, NEWS-018)
  3. Borealis agreement expiry. Retail Ledger (November 2025) reported expiry in 2026 or 2027. The July 22, 2026 amendment extends it to December 31, 2029. The news is superseded. (NEWS-002, SEC-006)
  4. Borealis share of revenue. The news says "about a third". Company data show 34.8% in FY2025, 39.3% in H1 2026 and 42.5% in Q2 2026. The news corroborates the data, which is more current. (NEWS-002, SEC-002, FIN-006)
  5. Fennimore royalty base. The company says its royalty method is "consistent with industry practice". Its own 10-K summary of Section 1.12 bars deductions for co-op, promotional allowances and chargebacks. (SEC-021, LIT-002, X-011)
  6. How co-op fees are treated. For revenue, co-op fees are a selling expense (SG&A), on the view that they buy advertising. For royalties, co-op is deducted from Net Sales. (SEC-008, SEC-021)
  7. EverTrail test results. The FTC alleges 29% to 46% recycled nylon. The nonprofit tests found 31% to 44%. These are different test sets, both allegations. (LIT-001, NEWS-004)
  8. Lease liabilities. The 10-K gives US$150.0m at December 31, 2025. The data pack gives US$152.0m. The difference is probably a date difference (not confirmed). (SEC-018, FIN-030)
  9. Deal brief against later sources. The brief (dated October 16, 2026) still assumes about 12% Borealis growth a year, although the July 22 amendment had already cut 2027 prices and raised co-op fees (X-004). The record 53.0% gross margin depends on the reserve release (X-001). The non-executive Chair plan triggers Fennimore Section 14.3 (X-010). Equity value leaves out the RSUs (X-017).

Questions for management

  1. Inventory reserve: Why was the method changed in Q2 2026, and who approved it? What would the old method give at June 30, 2026? Did the auditor review the change? What is the reserve at September 30, 2026?
  2. Borealis: What are the weeks of supply now, and what returns or lower reorders do you expect in H2 2026? What is the 2027 plan under the new terms? What volume growth offsets the 23.3 run-rate cost?
  3. PFAS: How much inventory and open purchase orders carry a PFAS finish? What is the plan to certify product for Borealis and Summitline by January 1, 2027? What reserve is held?
  4. EverTrail: Is the "100% Recycled Nylon" claim still in use? When did you learn of the Rio Verde invalidation? Did you receive the 2023 baseline report? Has a remedy range been computed?
  5. Fennimore: Has Fennimore been asked to approve a successor CEO or CCO? Would it approve a Westbrook candidate? Are the disputed deductions continuing in 2026, and how much has been underpaid so far?
  6. Founder: Would Ms. Achterberg support a Westbrook bid without a consulting arrangement? What role would she accept? Would she sign a non-compete?
  7. Meridian/FCPA: What is the investigation's scope? What contact has there been with the DOJ and SEC? Have the remaining brokers handled peak-season volume? Has ICFR been reassessed?
  8. Cedar Hollow: What is peak capacity under 12-hour operations? What backup capacity is there? What is the appeal route after November 18?
  9. Gift cards: What is the status of the Delaware audit? Could other states follow? What is the future breakage policy?
  10. Stores: What are store-level P&L and lease terms for the 13 stores with negative four-wall contribution? What impairment testing was done at December 31, 2025?
