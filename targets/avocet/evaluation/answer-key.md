# Project Avocet answer key

Target: Brindlewick Cold Chain, Inc. (BWCC), fictional. Hypothetical buyer: Trelliswood Logistics Corp. Deal brief dated October 28, 2026.

## Value base for every percentage test

**Every percentage in this key is a percentage of enterprise value (EV) of $2,750.0m**, unless it is labeled otherwise. The deal is priced on EV (11.0x LTM Q2 2026 Adjusted EBITDA of $250.0m), and Brindlewick carries $1,118.0m of net debt, so equity value ($1,632.0m) is only 59% of EV. Testing against equity value would inflate every percentage by about 1.7 times and push items across thresholds they do not cross on the price actually paid.

| Threshold | Test | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | more than 10% of EV | more than $275.0m |
| Known deal costs | 5% of EV | $137.5m |

A value effect expressed as earnings is converted at the deal's own multiple of 11.0x.

## The four test items

- **Test 1 (one value base; three kinds of known deal cost): P14.** Equity-award cash-out $46.2m + termination fee $49.0m + change-in-control severance $16.4m = **$111.6m = 4.06% of EV** (6.84% of equity value). A good report tests it against EV, rates it **Medium**, counts all three costs in the price, and lists the $72.0m of advisory, financing and other fees separately as a use of funds. Adding the fees would give $183.6m (6.68% of EV); adding only the $27.5m advisory fee would give $139.1m (5.06% of EV). Either addition is an error.
- **Test 2 (trend in the earnings the deal is priced on): P01, with the judgment as P05.** Adjusted EBITDA fell $21.0m half on half; annualized ($42.0m) at 11.0x that is **$462.0m = 16.8% of EV**, so **Critical**. Taken one at a time the metrics look smaller (the LTM base is only 7.7% below FY2025, which at 11.0x is 8.4% of EV). The January 1, 2026 cut in the claims accrual rate (P05) is one of the weakening metrics and is its own High issue. A good report shows the trend's value at the deal multiple, keeps P05 as its own risk-matrix row, and names P05 in the trend finding's sources and risk-matrix row, not only in a detail card.
- **Test 3 (full amount of an unrecorded liability): P02.** The full lease-end restoration obligation is **$312.0m = 11.35% of EV**, not recorded. The buyer's integration plan triggers four sites, **$118.0m = 4.29% of EV**. A good report applies the Critical test to the full $312.0m as the exposure the buyer takes on, rates it **Critical**, and shows $118.0m as the near-term cash cost of the integration plan (absent from the $45.0m integration budget).
- **Test 4 (unconfirmed claim the company denies): D4.** A single pseudonymous forum post alleges altered temperature records at Fresno; the company denied it on the record; no filing, court or regulatory record supports it. A good report rates it **Low**, lists it under open questions, gives it **no risk-matrix row** and asks for **no special deal protection**.
