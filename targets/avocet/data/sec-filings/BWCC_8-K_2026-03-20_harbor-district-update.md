## Item 8.01. Other events

On March 17, 2026, the Board of Commissioners of the Sandhaven Harbor District (the "District") adopted the Terminal 7 Modernization Plan. The plan designates the Terminal 7 uplands, which include the sites of Brindlewick Cold Chain, Inc.'s Sandhaven 1 and Sandhaven 2 refrigerated warehouses, for container yard and on-dock rail use beginning in 2029, subject to environmental review and funding.

Brindlewick operates Sandhaven 1 and Sandhaven 2 under a concession agreement with the District that expires on September 30, 2028 and contains no renewal option. On expiry, the improvements revert to the District. The two facilities have 118,000 pallet positions, about 10% of Brindlewick's warehouse capacity, and serve seafood and agricultural export customers using the Port of Sandhaven.

Brindlewick is in discussions with the District about the timing of the Terminal 7 plan, a possible extension of the concession, and alternative sites within the District. The plan adopted on March 17 does not itself terminate or amend the concession agreement. Brindlewick cannot predict the outcome of these discussions.
