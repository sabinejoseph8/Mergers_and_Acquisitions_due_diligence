**Warehousing.** Revenue declined 3.6%. Economic occupancy fell to 81.4% as frozen food customers reduced inventory levels and as new third-party capacity opened in the Central Valley. We granted storage rate concessions to retain volume at several facilities, and revenue per occupied pallet position declined 4.3%. Warehousing revenue is also net of $4.6 million of service credits issued to customers affected by the ColdTrack go-lives described below.

**Transportation.** Revenue declined 5.0% on lower loaded miles. The operating ratio worsened to 96.4%, reflecting driver wage increases and lower tractor utilization, partly offset by lower insurance and claims expense following the change in estimate described in Note 5.

**Value-added services.** Revenue grew 13.2%, driven by kitting and case-picking programs for grocery and life-sciences customers.

### ColdTrack warehouse management system

ColdTrack went live at Portland Rivergate in February 2026 and at Salem in April 2026, joining Fresno, which went live in October 2025. Both 2026 go-lives experienced inventory location and order accuracy problems. We issued $4.6 million of service credits in the first half of 2026, and two customers moved part of their volume to other providers. We have paused further deployments while we remediate the system. We now estimate total program cost at $61.0 million, compared with our original budget of $38.0 million. Capitalized software, which consists principally of ColdTrack costs, was $47.0 million at June 30, 2026. We assessed the capitalized costs for impairment and concluded that no impairment was required.

### Sandhaven

Sandhaven 1 and Sandhaven 2 contributed approximately $31.0 million, or approximately 12%, of our Adjusted EBITDA for the twelve months ended June 30, 2026. Their concession with the Sandhaven Harbor District expires on September 30, 2028. Discussions with the District about an extension or an alternative site continue. We have not reached agreement, and there can be no assurance that we will.

### Pricing

In July 2026 we entered into a seven-year warehousing agreement with a new customer for frozen storage at our Central Valley facilities. Two existing customers have since requested pricing reviews under the most-favored-customer provisions of their agreements. We believe the new agreement is not comparable to theirs in services or volume commitments.

### Energy

Our fixed-price electricity supply agreements expire on December 31, 2026. We have begun procurement of supply for 2027. Forward prices for 2027 delivery in our markets are substantially above the price under our current agreements.

### Liquidity and capital resources

At June 30, 2026 we had $95.0 million drawn on our revolving credit facility, $1,020.0 million of term loans outstanding (Term Loan A $300.0 million; Term Loan B $720.0 million), $77.0 million of finance lease liabilities and $74.0 million of cash. We were in compliance with all covenants. Maintenance capital expenditures were $15.6 million in the first half of 2026 (2.3% of revenue). We repurchased 515,000 shares for $14.0 million in the first quarter and suspended repurchases in April 2026.

## Part I, Item 1. Notes to condensed consolidated financial statements (excerpts)

### Note 5. Change in accounting estimate: insurance and claims

Effective January 1, 2026, we reduced the rate at which we accrue expected auto liability and cargo claims for the current accident year from 6.5% to 4.8% of Transportation revenue. The new rate reflects our assessment of the effect of the forward-facing and driver-facing camera telematics program we completed across the fleet in 2025. The change reduced insurance and claims expense by approximately $3.7 million, and increased net income by approximately $2.8 million, in the six months ended June 30, 2026. Accrued claims and insurance reserves were $44.1 million at June 30, 2026, compared with $46.5 million at December 31, 2025. Reserves for accident years before 2026 were not changed.

### Note 8. Equity awards

At June 30, 2026 there were outstanding 860,000 restricted stock units, 210,000 performance share units (at target) and options to purchase 1,180,000 shares at a weighted average exercise price of $25.68.

### Note 11. Commitments and contingencies

**Antitrust class action.** Defendants' motion to dismiss in *In re Refrigerated Warehousing Services Antitrust Litigation* remains under submission. We have not accrued a loss.

**Interstate 5 accident.** On April 17, 2026, the estates and families of three people killed in the February 3, 2025 Interstate 5 accident sued Brindlewick Transport LLC and the driver in San Joaquin County Superior Court. We have tendered the claim to our excess insurers and have reserved our $5.0 million retention.

**FreshArc Distributors.** FreshArc Distributors, Inc., a customer, filed for Chapter 11 protection on January 12, 2026 in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the District of Delaware. In July 2026 the liquidating trustee demanded the return of $14.6 million of payments we received in the 90 days before the petition date, as alleged preferential transfers. We believe we have ordinary course of business and new value defenses. We have not accrued a loss.

**Coastline Seafood Imports.** On May 27, 2026, Coastline Seafood Imports sued us for $41.2 million for product it alleges was spoiled after a compressor failure at Sandhaven 2 in February 2026. Our warehouse receipts limit our liability for loss of or damage to stored goods to $0.50 per pound unless the depositor declares a higher value and pays the related excess-value charge, which Coastline did not do. We have accrued $1.2 million, our estimate of liability under that limit.

**Calder Valley.** A hearing on the plaintiffs' motion for a preliminary injunction restricting nighttime truck operations at our Calder Valley facility is scheduled for January 15, 2027.

### Note 14. Subsequent events

On July 28, 2026, U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement served Notices of Inspection on six of our warehouses in California and Washington, requesting Forms I-9 and supporting records. In a self-audit begun in 2025, we identified unresolved discrepancies in employment eligibility documentation for approximately 410 employees. We are cooperating with the inspections. We cannot estimate any civil penalties or the effect on our workforce.
