## Item 5.02. Compensatory arrangements of certain officers

On September 21, 2026, the Board of Directors of Brindlewick Cold Chain, Inc. (the "Company"), on the recommendation of its Compensation Committee, adopted the Brindlewick Cold Chain, Inc. Change in Control Severance Plan (the "Plan"). The Plan covers the Company's nine executive officers and replaces the change-in-control severance provisions of their employment agreements.

Under the Plan, if a participant's employment is terminated by the Company without cause, or by the participant for good reason, within three months before or 24 months after a change in control, the participant will receive a lump-sum cash payment equal to a multiple of the sum of base salary and target annual bonus:

| Participant | Multiple |
|---|---|
| Chief Executive Officer | 2.5x |
| Other named executive officers (4) | 2.0x |
| Other executive officers (4) | 1.5x |

Payments are conditioned on a release of claims and on compliance with confidentiality and non-solicitation covenants. The Plan does not provide any excise tax gross-up; payments will be reduced to the extent that doing so would leave a participant better off after tax.

Base salaries and target bonus percentages for 2026 are unchanged from 2025. Based on current base salaries and target bonuses, the aggregate cash severance payable under the Plan if all nine executive officers had a qualifying termination would be approximately $16.4 million, of which approximately $11.7 million relates to the named executive officers.

Treatment of outstanding equity awards on a change in control continues to be governed by the 2020 Omnibus Incentive Plan.

The Plan is filed as Exhibit 10.1.

## Item 8.01. Other events

On September 22, 2026, the Company announced that its Board of Directors has begun a review of strategic alternatives to maximize shareholder value, including a sale of the Company, and has retained Ashcombe Partners as financial advisor and Calloway Reed LLP as legal counsel. The Company has not set a timetable for the review, and there can be no assurance that it will result in any transaction. The Company does not intend to comment further until the Board approves a specific transaction or concludes the review.

The closing price of the Company's common stock on September 21, 2026, the last trading day before the announcement, was $26.15.
