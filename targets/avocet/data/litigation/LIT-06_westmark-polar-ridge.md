## Background

Polar Ridge Logistics ran dedicated distribution for grocery chains in Northern California. Brindlewick sold Polar Ridge to Arcturus Freight Holdings, Inc. on June 30, 2023. Westmark alleges that Polar Ridge failed to meet service levels under a 2021 dedicated distribution agreement in 2022 and 2023, causing store stock-outs, and seeks to hold Brindlewick liable as Polar Ridge's parent at the time.

## Indemnity

Under Section 9.2 of the 2023 Membership Interest Purchase Agreement, Arcturus assumed all liabilities of Polar Ridge, whether arising before or after closing, and agreed to indemnify Brindlewick and its affiliates against any losses arising from Polar Ridge's business, without a cap or deductible for assumed liabilities. Brindlewick tendered its defense on October 20, 2025. Arcturus accepted the tender in writing on November 6, 2025, without reservation of rights, and its counsel now represents both defendants.

Arcturus Freight Holdings had revenue of about $4.1 billion in 2025 and carries investment-grade credit ratings (BBB from two agencies).

Brindlewick considers its net exposure remote and has not recorded an accrual.
