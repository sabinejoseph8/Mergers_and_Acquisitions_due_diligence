## Background

FreshArc Distributors, a food service distributor, was a Brindlewick warehousing and transportation customer from 2017. It was Brindlewick's tenth-largest customer in 2025, with revenue of $31.8 million. FreshArc's operations ceased in March 2026.

The trustee seeks to recover 41 payments totaling $14.6 million that FreshArc made to Brindlewick between October 14, 2025 and January 12, 2026, the 90 days before the petition date.

## Defenses

- **Ordinary course of business.** Over 2023 to mid-2025, FreshArc paid Brindlewick invoices in 32 to 40 days on average. During the preference period, payments were made 58 to 71 days after invoice, after Brindlewick placed FreshArc on credit hold in October 2025 and required payment before releasing inventory. The trustee argues that the credit hold and the changed payment timing take the payments outside the ordinary course.
- **Subsequent new value.** Brindlewick provided about $3.9 million of storage and transportation services after receiving preference-period payments, for which it was not paid. This would reduce exposure dollar for dollar if established.
- **Warehouse lien.** Brindlewick's counsel is evaluating whether payments made to release inventory subject to its warehouse lien are protected as transfers for which Brindlewick gave up a secured position.

Brindlewick's counsel estimates a likely recovery by the trustee of $6 million to $11 million if the case is not settled. Brindlewick has not recorded an accrual.
