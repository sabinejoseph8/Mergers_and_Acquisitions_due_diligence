## Background

The Calder Valley distribution center opened in June 2025 with 64,000 pallet positions on a ground-leased site. It serves grocery and food service customers in Southern California and contributed about 4% of Brindlewick's revenue in the twelve months ended June 30, 2026. Outbound loads are concentrated overnight for morning store delivery.

The county's 2023 land use approval permits 24-hour operation and requires noise at the property line to stay below 55 dBA at night. Plaintiffs' acoustic consultant recorded night-time peaks of 61 to 68 dBA at the nearest homes. Brindlewick's consultant recorded an hourly average of 53 dBA at the property line.

## Exposure

- **If an injunction issues as requested:** Brindlewick's operations team estimates that compressing dock activity into 6:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. would reduce the site's outbound capacity by about 35% until a second dock bank is built.
- **Mitigation:** a sound wall along the eastern boundary, an enclosed reefer plug-in yard and a revised truck route are estimated at $6.5 million.
- **Settlement:** in October 2026 the Alliance's counsel indicated it would accept an 11:00 p.m. to 5:00 a.m. restriction plus the sound wall.
