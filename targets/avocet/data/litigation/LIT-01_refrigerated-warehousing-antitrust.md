## Allegations

Coldmetric Analytics ran a subscription benchmarking service for refrigerated warehouse operators. Subscribers submitted current storage and handling rates by facility and customer, and received weekly reports showing rates by market and customer-size band. Plaintiffs allege that the reports were current, granular enough to identify competitors' pricing to specific customer segments, and available only to participating operators, and that defendants used them to stabilize and raise rates from 2019. The complaint cites internal emails in which pricing managers at two defendants refer to "holding the Coldmetric line."

Brindlewick subscribed to Coldmetric from 2018 until Coldmetric suspended its rate reports in March 2025.

## Damages

Plaintiffs' consulting economist estimates class-wide overcharges of at least $600 million before trebling. The complaint attributes about 8% of class purchases to Brindlewick. Single damages attributable to Brindlewick's own sales on that basis would be about $48 million, or about $144 million trebled. Defendants are jointly and severally liable for the full trebled amount, with no right of contribution under federal antitrust law.

## Settlements to date

Northern Frost Storage, the smallest defendant (about 2% of class purchases per the complaint), settled in July 2026 for $9.5 million plus cooperation, subject to court approval.

## Counsel's assessment (from Brindlewick's litigation summary provided to bidders)

Hartwell Brooke considers the information-exchange theory viable after the August 14 order and expects class certification to be contested on damages methodology. The firm has not provided a loss estimate. Brindlewick has not recorded an accrual.
