# Brindlewick cuts 2026 outlook as occupancy slides; shares drop 12%

**FICTIONAL.** This article and every company, person and event in it are invented.

*Capital Markets Wire | August 7, 2026*

TACOMA, Wash.: Brindlewick Cold Chain, Inc. (NYSE: BWCC) cut its full-year earnings outlook on Thursday after first-half Adjusted EBITDA fell 15.1% to $118.0 million from $139.0 million.

The company now expects 2026 Adjusted EBITDA of $236 million to $244 million, down from $268 million to $276 million in February. Shares fell 12.0% to $24.20.

Warehouse economic occupancy averaged 81.4% in the first half, down from 86.1% a year earlier, and revenue per occupied pallet fell as Brindlewick cut rates to keep customers. The transportation operating ratio rose to 96.4% from 93.8%.

"We don't see the second half looking very different from the first," Chief Financial Officer Priya Venkataraman told analysts. "Customers are carrying less inventory and there is more capacity in our markets than there was a year ago."

Several analysts questioned the company's lower insurance and claims accrual rate, which cut expense by about $3.7 million in the half. "Accident frequency is going up and the accrual rate is going down," said Bettina Vos of Harrow Equity Research. "Those two lines usually move together." Venkataraman said the change reflected "measurable results from the camera program" and that reserves for prior years were unchanged.

Value-added services revenue grew 13.2%.

Chief Executive Daniel Achterberg declined to comment on market speculation that Brindlewick could attract takeover interest.
