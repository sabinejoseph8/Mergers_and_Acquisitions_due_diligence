# Brindlewick donates freezer space and trucks to county food bank

**FICTIONAL.** This article and every company, person and event in it are invented.

*Sandhaven Harbor Ledger | October 14, 2026 | By Martin Oyelaran*

Brindlewick Cold Chain will give the Harbor County Food Bank 400 pallet positions of free frozen storage at its Sandhaven 2 warehouse through the end of the year, and two refrigerated trucks for weekly deliveries to rural pantries.

Food bank director Lucille Amankwah said the donation would let the bank accept 90,000 pounds of frozen turkey and fish it would otherwise have turned away during the holidays. "Freezer space is the thing we can never get enough of," Amankwah said.

Brindlewick employees will also volunteer at the food bank's Thanksgiving distribution on November 21. The company has supported the food bank since 2014.
