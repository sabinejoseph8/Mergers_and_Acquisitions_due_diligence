# Western power forwards for 2027 hold near record as data center demand bites

**FICTIONAL.** This article and every company, person and event in it are invented.

*Grid & Power Report | September 30, 2026 | By Nils Haugen*

Forward prices for 2027 electricity delivery in the Pacific Northwest and Northern California finished the third quarter close to record levels, leaving industrial buyers that need to lock in supply for next year facing sharply higher bills.

Calendar-2027 baseload forwards at the Mid-Columbia hub settled at about $97 per megawatt-hour on September 29, and Northern California forwards at about $111, according to broker quotes. Both are roughly 60% to 80% above where the same contracts traded in 2021.

Retail suppliers said fixed-price offers to large industrial customers for 2027 through 2029 delivery, including transmission and shaping, were averaging about $104 per megawatt-hour in Washington and California. "Anyone rolling off a contract signed in 2021 is looking at a step-up of 60% or more," said Corinne Vale of Westshore Energy Advisors. "Cold storage, food processing and data centers are all in the same queue."

Analysts attribute the rise to data center load growth, lower hydropower output after two dry winters and the retirement of gas plants in California.

Buyers that wait may not fare better: the futures curve slopes upward through 2029, Vale said.
