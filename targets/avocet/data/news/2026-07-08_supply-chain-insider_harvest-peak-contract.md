# Brindlewick lands Harvest Peak Foods frozen storage deal with sharp pricing

**FICTIONAL.** This article and every company, person and event in it are invented.

*Supply Chain Insider | July 8, 2026 | By Theo Marchetti*

Brindlewick Cold Chain has signed a seven-year agreement to store frozen vegetables and fruit for Harvest Peak Foods at its Stockton North, Modesto East and Lodi warehouses, filling about 30,000 pallet positions that have sat empty since spring.

Two people familiar with the bidding said Brindlewick won the business with storage rates about 9% below its standard Central Valley rate card, undercutting two rivals. Brindlewick Senior Vice President of Sales Gregory Halloran did not dispute the figure. "We priced to fill capacity in a soft market," Halloran said. "Harvest Peak is a growing customer and seven years of committed volume is worth a lot to us."

Harvest Peak, which is consolidating three processing plants into a new facility near Turlock, moves its first pallets into Brindlewick buildings in September.

Occupancy across the Central Valley has fallen this year as new speculative cold storage buildings have opened in Stockton and Patterson, adding about 140,000 pallet positions since 2024, according to Coldmetric Analytics.

One food company executive whose products are stored by Brindlewick said the price "will not go unnoticed" by existing customers. "Everyone in this valley talks to everyone," the executive said.
