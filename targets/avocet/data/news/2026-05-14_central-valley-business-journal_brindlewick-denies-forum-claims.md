# Brindlewick denies anonymous online claim about Fresno temperature records

**FICTIONAL.** This article and every company, person and event in it are invented.

*Central Valley Business Journal | May 14, 2026 | By Renata Solis*

Brindlewick Cold Chain has denied an anonymous post on an industry message board alleging that supervisors at its Fresno warehouse altered temperature records for pharmaceutical storage rooms.

The post, published May 11 on the ColdChainTalk forum by a user who joined that day and has posted nothing else, claimed that temperature "spikes" in refrigerated rooms used by a pharmaceutical distributor were deleted from reports sent to the customer in 2025. The poster offered no documents and did not identify the customer. Forum moderators locked the thread two days later.

"The allegation is false," said Marisa Thornquist, Brindlewick's vice president of communications. "Temperature data in our controlled rooms is recorded by independent wireless loggers that write directly to the customer's own monitoring platform. Brindlewick employees cannot edit or delete those records." Thornquist said the company's life-sciences customers audit the Fresno rooms at least twice a year and that the most recent audit, in March, found no deviations.

Alder BioPharma Distribution, the only pharmaceutical distributor that Brindlewick lists as a major customer, said in an email that it "has no concerns regarding Brindlewick's temperature-controlled storage" and continues to use the Fresno facility.

The Business Journal found no lawsuit, regulatory warning letter or state inspection finding involving temperature records at the Fresno facility. The California Board of Pharmacy said it had no public enforcement record involving Brindlewick.

The Business Journal was unable to identify or contact the author of the post.
