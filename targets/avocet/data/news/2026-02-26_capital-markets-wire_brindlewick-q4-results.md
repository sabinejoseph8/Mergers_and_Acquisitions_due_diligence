# Brindlewick posts record 2025 earnings, guides higher for 2026

**FICTIONAL.** This article and every company, person and event in it are invented.

*Capital Markets Wire | February 26, 2026*

TACOMA, Wash.: Brindlewick Cold Chain, Inc. (NYSE: BWCC) reported record Adjusted EBITDA of $271.0 million for 2025, up 7.5% from $252.0 million, on revenue of $1.398 billion, up 4.2%.

Value-added services, the company's smallest segment, again led growth: revenue rose 17.4% to $182.0 million as grocers and life-sciences distributors outsourced kitting and case picking. "Value-added services has compounded at more than 18% a year since 2022, and it lives inside buildings we already operate," Chief Executive Daniel Achterberg said on the earnings call.

The company guided 2026 Adjusted EBITDA to $268 million to $276 million. Chief Financial Officer Priya Venkataraman said the guidance assumes warehouse occupancy "in the mid-80s" and a full year of the Calder Valley and Bayfront facilities.

Net income fell to $57.1 million from $81.9 million, because 2024 included a $46.0 million gain from the sale and leaseback of five warehouses.

Asked about the company's Sandhaven port facilities, whose concession expires in 2028, Achterberg said Brindlewick "has a long and constructive relationship with the Harbor District" and expected to reach an extension.

Shares rose 3.1% to $27.22.
