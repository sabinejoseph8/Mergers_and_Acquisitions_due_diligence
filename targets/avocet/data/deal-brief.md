# Project Avocet: deal brief

**FICTIONAL.** All companies, people, figures and events are invented. The transaction is hypothetical.

*Prepared by Trelliswood Corporate Development for the Investment Committee, October 28, 2026. Confidential.*

## 1. The proposal

Trelliswood Logistics Corp. (NYSE: TWLC, "Trelliswood") proposes to acquire 100% of Brindlewick Cold Chain, Inc. (NYSE: BWCC, "Brindlewick") for **$34.00 per share in cash** by a one-step merger.

Brindlewick announced a review of strategic alternatives on September 22, 2026. Trelliswood submitted a non-binding indication of $33.00 to $35.00 per share on October 9, 2026 and was invited to the second round. Final proposals with a marked merger agreement are due November 20, 2026. We ask the Committee to approve a binding proposal at $34.00.

## 2. Deal model basis

The deal model values Brindlewick at **11.0x Adjusted EBITDA (company definition) for the twelve months ended June 30, 2026 of $250.0m**, which gives the enterprise value of $2,750.0m above. The LTM figure is built from Brindlewick's published FY2025 and first-half results (FY2025 $271.0m, less H1 2025 $139.0m, plus H1 2026 $118.0m). Synergies are excluded from the price and are treated as Trelliswood's upside.

The 11.0x multiple sits between the public trading range for refrigerated logistics peers (9.5x to 11.5x LTM) and the two most recent sector transactions (12.0x and 12.5x).

## 4. Proposed key terms

- **Termination fee payable by Brindlewick:** $49.0m (3.0% of equity value), payable if Brindlewick accepts a superior proposal or the board changes its recommendation.
- **Reverse termination fee payable by Trelliswood:** $110.0m (4.0% of enterprise value), payable if the merger fails for antitrust reasons.
- **Regulatory efforts:** Trelliswood will agree to divest facilities that generated, in aggregate, up to $15.0m of LTM Adjusted EBITDA. No obligation beyond that.
- **Outside date:** 12 months after signing, extendable once by 3 months if only antitrust clearance is outstanding.
- **Financing:** fully committed bridge facility from three banks; no financing condition.
- **Employees:** Trelliswood will honor Brindlewick's Change in Control Severance Plan adopted September 2026. Trelliswood's management team will run the combined business, and we expect all nine Brindlewick executive officers to leave within three months of closing.

## 5. Integration plan

Trelliswood operates 23 refrigerated warehouses, including six in the Puget Sound market and five in the Northern Central Valley of California. Brindlewick's network overlaps ours in both markets.

The plan, consistent with the Network 2028 program presented at our June 2026 investor day:

- **Consolidate four Brindlewick leased warehouses into existing Trelliswood facilities within 24 months of closing:** Puyallup Junction (WA), Kent Valley (WA), Stockton North (CA) and Lodi (CA). Customers move to Trelliswood buildings in the same markets, and the four Brindlewick leases are surrendered.
- Migrate Brindlewick sites onto Trelliswood's warehouse management system and halt Brindlewick's ColdTrack rollout.
- Combine the two trucking fleets under Trelliswood's safety and dispatch programs.

Run-rate synergies: **$38.0m** a year by year three ($21.0m network consolidation, $17.0m overhead). One-time integration costs: **$45.0m** (product moves, racking, IT migration, non-executive severance).

## 7. Questions for the Investment Committee

1. **Earnings base.** Is $250.0m of LTM Adjusted EBITDA a sound base for an 11.0x multiple, given Brindlewick's first-half 2026 results?
2. **Unrecorded obligations.** Are there obligations outside net debt, or not on Brindlewick's balance sheet, that we would inherit or that our integration plan would set off?
3. **Antitrust.** Can the combination clear review within our proposed divestiture limit and outside date?
4. **Cost of the deal.** What will the transaction cost us beyond the headline equity price, and how should those costs be presented against the value of the deal?
5. **Contracts and facilities.** Which leases, supply contracts and customer contracts change economics after 2026 or on a change of control?
6. **Litigation and compliance.** Which pending matters need a price adjustment, a specific protection in the merger agreement, or further work before signing?
