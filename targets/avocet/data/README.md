# Project Avocet data room

**FICTIONAL.** Brindlewick Cold Chain, Inc., Trelliswood Logistics Corp., every other company, every person, every court case and every number in this folder are invented. Any resemblance to real companies, people or proceedings is coincidental. The deal described here is hypothetical.

## The target

Brindlewick Cold Chain, Inc. (NYSE: BWCC, fictional) is a temperature-controlled logistics company headquartered in Tacoma, Washington. It operates 19 refrigerated warehouses in Washington, Oregon, California and Idaho, a refrigerated trucking fleet, and value-added services such as blast freezing, case picking and kitting. Fiscal year ends December 31.
