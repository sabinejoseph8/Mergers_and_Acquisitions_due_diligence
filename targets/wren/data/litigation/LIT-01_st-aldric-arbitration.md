## The contract

- Fixed-price agreement dated January 22, 2024 to modernize 46 elevators at St. Aldric's main campus in Springfield, Illinois. Price: **US$61.4 million**.
- Substantial completion was due March 31, 2026. Current forecast: February 2027.
- Liquidated damages of US$50,000 per day of delay after March 31, 2026, capped at 25% of the contract price (**US$15.4 million**).
- Consequential damages are excluded "except to the extent caused by the gross negligence or willful misconduct of Contractor."

## The claim

| Component | US$ millions |
|---|---|
| Liquidated damages through forecast completion (about 320 days at US$50,000, claimed without the cap) | 16.0 |
| Temporary patient transport, diverted procedures and lost revenue | 17.7 |
| Third-party consultant and completion costs | 4.3 |
| **Total** | **38.0** |

St. Aldric alleges that Meridale reassigned its project crew to other jobs in late 2025 and misrepresented progress, which it says is gross negligence that removes the cap and the consequential damages exclusion.

## Meridale's position

- Delays were caused largely by St. Aldric's late access to elevator banks and two design changes.
- Liquidated damages accrue at most to the cap, and consequential damages are barred.
- Q2 2026: Meridale recorded a **US$5.2 million** loss provision on the contract (estimated costs at completion now exceed the price). No amount is accrued for liquidated damages.
- Outside counsel's preliminary view (September 2026): exposure to liquidated damages "likely, at least in part"; gross negligence finding "possible but less likely than not."

## Insurance

Contract delay claims are not covered by Meridale's general liability program.
