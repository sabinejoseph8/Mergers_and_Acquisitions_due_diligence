## Allegations

1. Meridale's standard contracts renew automatically for five-year terms. The New Jersey Service Contract Renewal Act requires a written reminder 30 to 60 days before the cancellation window for renewal terms longer than one year. Plaintiff alleges Meridale sent no reminders before 2025.
2. Early termination fees of 50% of the remaining contract value are a penalty, not liquidated damages.
3. Renewals made without the required notice are voidable at the customer's option.

## Meridale's position and exposure

- The Act's application to commercial buildings is contested. Meridale's motion argues that most class members are sophisticated commercial owners.
- Meridale began sending renewal reminders in New Jersey in March 2025.
- New Jersey represented about 9% of maintenance revenue in FY2025 (about US$53 million). About 71% of company-wide maintenance contracts are on five-year evergreen terms.
- Cancellation fees recognized company-wide were US$9.8 million in FY2025 and US$6.2 million in H1 2026.
- No accrual. The company says a range of loss cannot be estimated.
