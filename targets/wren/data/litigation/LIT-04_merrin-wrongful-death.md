## Facts

On June 14, 2025, a step chain failed on an escalator at Harbor Pointe Mall in Scottsdale, Arizona. Lucas Merrin, 67, fell and later died of his injuries. Meridale maintained the escalator under a standard contract with the mall owner, Calder Mall Partners. The Arizona elevator safety inspector's report found a worn step chain that should have been flagged at the April 2025 maintenance visit.
