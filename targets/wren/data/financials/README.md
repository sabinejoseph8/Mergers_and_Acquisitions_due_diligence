# Meridale Lift Services: financial data files

**FICTIONAL. All figures are invented for testing.**

US$ millions unless a row says otherwise. Fiscal year is the calendar year. H1 columns are the six months ended June 30. Figures come from the 10-K for fiscal 2025 and the 10-Q for the quarter ended June 30, 2026. The H1 2025 and H1 2026 columns are unaudited.

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `income_statement.csv` | FY2022 to FY2025, H1 2025, H1 2026. Revenue is shown by segment (rows starting `Revenue:`). Depreciation sits inside cost of revenue and SG&A. Amortization of capitalized contract acquisition costs (sales commissions) sits in SG&A. |
| `balance_sheet.csv` | December 31, 2023, 2024 and 2025, and June 30, 2026 |
| `cash_flow.csv` | FY2023 to FY2025, H1 2025, H1 2026 |
| `adjusted_ebitda_reconciliation.csv` | Company definition of Adjusted EBITDA, FY2022 to LTM Q2 2026. LTM = FY2025 - H1 2025 + H1 2026. |
| `revenue_quarterly.csv` | Total revenue by quarter, Q1 2022 to Q2 2026 |
| `revenue_by_customer.csv` | Ten largest customers by FY2025 revenue |
| `kpis.csv` | Operating metrics from MD&A and investor materials. The last column is June 30, 2026 for period-end figures and the trailing 12 months (or H1 2026 annualized where the row says so) for rates. |

## Notes

- Pinecrest Lift Co. was acquired on March 3, 2025 for US$152.0 million, net of cash acquired. Its results are included from that date.
- Other income (expense) net in FY2024 includes a US$41.2 million loss on extinguishment of debt: a US$38.2 million redemption premium on the 8.25% notes due 2027 and a US$3.0 million write-off of unamortized issuance costs.
- H1 2026 cost of revenue includes a US$4.1 million out-of-period adjustment recorded in Q1 2026 and a US$5.2 million provision for an estimated loss on a fixed-price modernization contract recorded in Q2 2026. See the 10-Q.
- Long-term debt, net is principal less unamortized issuance costs. At June 30, 2026, principal was US$645.0 million: term loan US$245.0 million (US$2.5 million current) and 5.875% senior notes due 2030 US$400.0 million. The revolving credit facility was undrawn.
- Deferred tax assets are shown net. They include tax-effected federal net operating loss carryforwards. See the 10-K income tax note.
- The investment in Laurent-Meridale Elevateurs (50%, equity method, US$9.7 million at June 30, 2026) is in other long-term assets.
