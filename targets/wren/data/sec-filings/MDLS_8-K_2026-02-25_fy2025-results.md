## Exhibit 99.1: Meridale reports record fiscal 2025 results

INDIANAPOLIS, February 25, 2026. Meridale Lift Services, Inc. (NYSE: MDLS) today reported results for the fourth quarter and fiscal year ended December 31, 2025.

**Fiscal 2025 highlights**

- Revenue of US$1,067.7 million, up 8.5%, with organic growth of 5.4%(1)
- Connected services revenue up 38.1% to US$35.9 million; 52,800 connected units
- Adjusted EBITDA(2) of US$186.4 million, up 5.3%, a record
- Net income of US$62.9 million, or US$1.35 per diluted share
- Units under contract of 176,300, up 6.9%, including the Pinecrest acquisition

**Fourth quarter highlights**

- Revenue of US$277.1 million, up 9.9% from US$252.1 million

"2025 was a record year," said Dorian Pell, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer. "Organic growth accelerated, Pinecrest is fully integrated, and LiftSense is changing how our customers think about uptime."

**Fiscal 2026 outlook**

- Revenue of US$1,095 million to US$1,120 million
- Adjusted EBITDA of US$192 million to US$198 million
