## Item 1.01 Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement

On September 20, 2026, Meridale Lift Services, Inc. (the "Company") entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger (the "Merger Agreement") with Wren Parent Holdings, LLC ("Parent") and Wren Merger Sub, Inc. ("Merger Sub"), affiliates of Ostrander Hale Partners Fund IV, L.P. Merger Sub will merge with and into the Company, and the Company will survive as a wholly owned subsidiary of Parent.

**Merger consideration.** Each share of common stock outstanding at the effective time (other than shares held by Parent, the rollover holder or stockholders who properly demand appraisal under Section 262 of the Delaware General Corporation Law) will be converted into the right to receive US$41.50 in cash.

**Equity awards.** At the effective time:
- each restricted stock unit, vested or unvested, will be cancelled for cash equal to US$41.50 per underlying share;
- each performance stock unit will vest at target and be cancelled for cash equal to US$41.50 per share at target; and
- each stock option will be cancelled for cash equal to the excess of US$41.50 over its exercise price.

**Conditions.** Closing is subject to:
- adoption of the Merger Agreement by holders of a majority of the outstanding shares;
- adoption by holders of a majority of the outstanding shares not held by Parent, the rollover holder, the Company's directors and executive officers, or their affiliates (the "Unaffiliated Vote");
- expiration or termination of the HSR Act waiting period;
- **holders of not more than 10% of the outstanding shares having demanded appraisal** (a condition for the benefit of Parent only, which Parent may waive); and
- other customary conditions.

**Brockmere.** The Company has agreed to terminate its agreement to acquire Brockmere Elevator Co. before closing, at Parent's request, and to pay any termination fee payable under that agreement.

**Termination fees.** The Company must pay Parent a termination fee of US$57.5 million if the Merger Agreement is terminated in certain circumstances, including to accept a superior proposal or after a change of the board's recommendation. Parent must pay the Company a reverse termination fee of US$115.0 million in certain circumstances, including a financing failure.

**Financing.** Parent has obtained equity commitments from Ostrander Hale Partners Fund IV and debt commitments for a US$925.0 million senior secured term loan. The Company's 5.875% senior notes due 2030 are expected to remain outstanding after closing. The Company has agreed to cooperate with any change-of-control offer for the notes required by the indenture. There is no financing condition.

**Rollover.** Dorian Pell, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer, has agreed to contribute 450,000 shares to Parent in exchange for Parent equity and has entered into a voting agreement.

**Special committee.** The Merger Agreement was approved by the board on the unanimous recommendation of a special committee of independent directors. Mr. Pell did not participate in the deliberations.

## Item 8.01 Other Events

On September 21, 2026, the Company and Ostrander Hale issued a joint press release announcing the merger. The price represents a premium of 25.0% to the Company's closing price of US$33.20 on September 18, 2026, the last trading day before press reports of a possible transaction.
