#### Note 11. Commitments and contingencies (excerpts)

*Code compliance.* On April 1, 2026, New Jersey adopted a rule requiring door-lock monitoring and car door restrictors on passenger elevators by July 1, 2029, similar to the Illinois Elevator Door Integrity Act (deadline December 31, 2028). At June 30, 2026, about **22,400 units** were under comprehensive contracts in Illinois and New Jersey, most on contract forms that do not exclude code-mandated upgrades. We have not completed our estimate of the cost of compliance. We recognize these costs as incurred and have not recorded a liability. We intend to seek recovery of a portion through price adjustments, which are subject to the escalator caps in many of these contracts.

*Fixed-price modernization contract.* In the second quarter of 2026, we recorded a **US$5.2 million provision** for the estimated loss on a US$61.4 million fixed-price contract to modernize 46 elevators at the main campus of a healthcare customer. Substantial completion was due March 31, 2026 and is now expected in February 2027. On July 14, 2026, the customer, St. Aldric Health System, filed a demand for arbitration seeking US$38.0 million, including liquidated damages and delay-related damages. The contract caps liquidated damages at 25% of the contract price (US$15.4 million) and excludes consequential damages except in cases of gross negligence or willful misconduct, which the customer alleges. We believe we have meritorious defenses, including customer-caused delays, and have not accrued any amount for liquidated damages because we do not consider a loss probable and cannot reasonably estimate it.

*Automatic renewal matters.* On March 30, 2026, we received a civil investigative demand from the New Jersey Division of Consumer Affairs about the renewal notices and early termination fees in our maintenance contracts. On May 19, 2026, Reyes Property Group LLC filed a putative class action in New Jersey Superior Court on behalf of New Jersey customers, alleging that our automatic renewal and cancellation fee practices violate the New Jersey Service Contract Renewal Act and seeking refunds of cancellation fees, treble damages and an injunction. We are cooperating with the investigation and intend to defend the lawsuit. We cannot estimate a range of possible loss.

*Vantor Elevator.* On May 12, 2026, the U.S. District Court for the Southern District of Indiana dismissed Vantor Elevator Co.'s trade secret claims against us with prejudice. Vantor did not appeal within the time allowed.

*Brockmere acquisition.* On June 15, 2026, we agreed to acquire Brockmere Elevator Co., a privately held service company in Ohio and Pennsylvania, for US$96.0 million in cash, subject to adjustments. Closing is expected in the fourth quarter of 2026. If we terminate the agreement before December 31, 2026 for any reason other than a breach by the sellers, we must pay the sellers a termination fee of **US$9.5 million**.

#### Collective agreement

On June 11, 2026, Lift Mechanics members ratified a new four-year national agreement effective July 1, 2026. It provides combined wage and benefit increases of **6.5% a year** for each of the four years, compared with an average of 3.4% a year under the prior agreement. Wages and benefits for employees covered by the agreement were about US$286 million in fiscal 2025. About 62% of our maintenance contracts by revenue cap annual price escalators at the lesser of CPI and 4.0%.

#### Connected services

Our wireless carrier for LiftSense, Northline Wireless, has announced that it will shut down its LTE-M network on September 30, 2027. About 9,800 connected units use LTE-M modules. Under our LiftSense terms, we replace hardware at our cost. We estimate replacement at about US$530 per unit (US$340 for hardware and US$190 for labor), or about US$5.2 million, most of it in 2027.

#### Liquidity

Our interest rate swap on US$200.0 million of the term loan, fixing SOFR at 1.92%, expires December 31, 2026. At current forward rates, interest expense on the term loan would rise by about US$4.2 million a year after expiry.
