## Summary

You are being asked to vote on a proposal to adopt the merger agreement under which Meridale would be acquired by affiliates of Ostrander Hale Partners Fund IV for US$41.50 per share in cash.

**Required votes.** Adoption requires (1) the affirmative vote of a majority of the outstanding shares and (2) the affirmative vote of a majority of the outstanding shares held by unaffiliated stockholders. Unaffiliated stockholders exclude Parent, Mr. Pell, the Company's other directors and executive officers, and their affiliates, who together hold about 1,430,000 shares (3.1%). Failing to vote has the same effect as voting against.

**Appraisal rights.** Stockholders who do not vote for the merger and who comply with Section 262 of the DGCL may seek a court determination of the fair value of their shares, which may be more or less than US$41.50. Parent's obligation to close is conditioned on holders of not more than 10% of the outstanding shares having demanded appraisal.

## Background of the merger (excerpts)

- **March 2026.** Halsey Rock Capital Management, then holding about 9% of the shares, asked for two board seats and urged the board to consider a sale. The board added one independent director.
- **May 22, 2026.** Ostrander Hale delivered a non-binding indication of interest at US$44.00 to US$46.00 per share.
- **July 9, 2026.** The board formed a special committee of four independent directors, chaired by Helen Ashgrove.
- **July 16, 2026.** The special committee engaged Pellham Rowe & Co. as financial advisor.
- **August 6, 2026.** The Company reported second-quarter results and lowered its fiscal 2026 outlook. The share price fell 21% to US$31.90.
- **August 20, 2026.** Ostrander Hale revised its indication to US$39.00 per share, citing second-quarter results.
- **August and September 2026.** At the special committee's direction, Pellham Rowe contacted six strategic and nine financial parties. Three signed confidentiality agreements. None made a proposal.
- **September 14, 2026.** Ostrander Hale raised its price to US$41.50 as its best and final offer, conditioned on termination of the Brockmere agreement and on the 10% appraisal condition.
- **September 20, 2026.** The special committee recommended, and the board approved, the merger agreement.

## Opinion of Pellham Rowe & Co. (excerpts)

Pellham Rowe delivered its opinion that, as of September 20, 2026, the US$41.50 per share consideration was fair, from a financial point of view, to the unaffiliated stockholders. Its discounted cash flow analysis of the August 2026 management case implied a range of US$38.10 to US$46.80 per share.

*Fees and relationships.* The Company will pay Pellham Rowe a fee of US$31.5 million, of which US$4.5 million became payable on delivery of its opinion and **US$27.0 million is contingent on closing**. In the two years before its engagement, Pellham Rowe received about US$14.2 million in fees from Ostrander Hale and its portfolio companies. An affiliate, Pellham Rowe Credit Partners, is a joint lead arranger of the US$925.0 million term loan commitment to Parent and expects to receive fees of about US$7.9 million. The special committee was informed of these relationships before engaging Pellham Rowe.

## Financing of the merger (excerpt)

Parent expects to fund the merger with US$1,304.9 million of equity from Fund IV, the rollover of Mr. Pell's shares, a US$925.0 million term loan and cash on hand. The Company's 5.875% senior notes due 2030 (US$400.0 million) are expected to remain outstanding. The merger will be a change of control under the indenture. Within 30 days after closing, the Company must offer to repurchase all notes at 101% of principal plus accrued interest. The debt commitment does not include financing for any such repurchase.

## Regulatory approvals and third-party consents

The Federal Trade Commission granted early termination of the HSR waiting period on October 14, 2026. Because the merger is a reverse triangular merger in which the Company survives, the Company does not expect it to require the consent of customers under contracts that restrict assignment. The Company is not aware of any material customer contract with a change-of-control provision.

Groupe Laurent Inc. has told the Company that it is reviewing its rights under the Laurent-Meridale partnership agreement, which allow it to require the Company to buy its 50% interest after a change of control.
