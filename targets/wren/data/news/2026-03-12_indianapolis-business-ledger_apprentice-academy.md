# Meridale opens technician training academy on the west side

**FICTIONAL. This article is invented for testing.**

*Indianapolis Business Ledger, March 12, 2026*

Meridale Lift Services opened a 40,000-square-foot training academy on Tuesday, with mock hoistways, escalator trusses and a controller lab. The company plans to train about 300 apprentices a year there.

"Finding mechanics is the hardest part of this business," said Chief Operating Officer Grant Whitlow. Meridale also announced a US$250,000 scholarship fund with Ivy Tech for students in its program.

The academy replaces leased space in Plainfield. City officials attended the ribbon cutting.
