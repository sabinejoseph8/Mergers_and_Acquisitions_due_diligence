# Halsey Rock says it will vote against Meridale buyout and seek appraisal

**FICTIONAL. This article is invented for testing.**

*Capital Markets Wire, October 6, 2026*

Halsey Rock Capital Management, Meridale Lift Services' largest holder with 11.4%, said it will vote against the US$41.50-a-share sale to Ostrander Hale and, if the deal is approved, demand appraisal of all its shares in Delaware.

In a letter to the board, Halsey Rock partner Owen Treadwell called the price "opportunistic, struck at the bottom of a single bad half-year," and cited reports that Ostrander Hale had discussed US$44 to US$46 a share earlier in the year. Bramford Investors, which owns 5.8%, said separately it was "leaning against."

The merger agreement lets Ostrander Hale walk away if holders of more than 10% of the shares seek appraisal. "Halsey Rock alone is over that line," said a merger arbitrage trader. Meridale shares closed at US$38.60, a wide discount to the deal price.

Meridale's 2030 notes traded at 98.5 cents on the dollar, below the 101 holders can demand once the deal closes.
