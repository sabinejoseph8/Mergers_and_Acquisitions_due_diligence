# Lift Mechanics pension fund slides to "critical and declining"

**FICTIONAL. This article is invented for testing.**

*Pension and Benefits Monitor, May 27, 2026*

The Lift Mechanics National Pension Fund has been certified in critical and declining status for 2026, with projected insolvency in 2041. The fund was 58% funded at January 1, 2026.

Contributing employers include the large elevator manufacturers' service units and Meridale Lift Services, the largest independent. Withdrawal liability estimates sent to employers this spring rose about 14% from last year, according to two employers.

Under federal law, an employer that stops contributing for all or part of its covered workforce owes its share of unfunded vested benefits. "Converting a branch to non-union is a partial withdrawal," said benefits attorney Carla Mendes. "Employers sometimes discover that only after a deal closes."
