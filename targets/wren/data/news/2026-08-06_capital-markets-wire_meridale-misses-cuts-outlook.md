*Capital Markets Wire, August 6, 2026*

Meridale Lift Services shares fell 21% to US$31.90 after the company reported first-half Adjusted EBITDA down 16.9% to US$74.7 million and withdrew its fiscal 2026 guidance range in favor of "about US$168 million."
