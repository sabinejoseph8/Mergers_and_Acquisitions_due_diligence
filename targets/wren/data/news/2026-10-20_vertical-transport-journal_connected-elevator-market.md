# Connected elevators: monitoring market to triple by 2030, study says

**FICTIONAL. This article is invented for testing.**

*Vertical Transport Journal, October 20, 2026*

Remote monitoring subscriptions for elevators in North America will grow about 25% a year to 2030, according to a study by Halvorsen Research. Manufacturers lead the market, but independents such as Meridale's LiftSense are growing faster from a smaller base.

The study flags one near-term hurdle: older monitoring devices on carrier networks that are shutting down. Northline Wireless will turn off its LTE-M network on September 30, 2027, and Halvorsen estimates 30,000 elevator monitoring devices in the U.S. still use it.

Pricing has held at US$25 to US$45 per unit per month, the study found.
