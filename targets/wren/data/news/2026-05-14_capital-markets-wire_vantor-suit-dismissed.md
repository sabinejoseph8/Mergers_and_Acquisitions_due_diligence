# Court tosses Vantor trade secret case against Meridale

**FICTIONAL. This article is invented for testing.**

*Capital Markets Wire, May 14, 2026*

A federal judge in Indianapolis dismissed Vantor Elevator Co.'s trade secret lawsuit against Meridale Lift Services with prejudice on May 12. Vantor had sought US$22 million, alleging that technicians who moved to Meridale took customer route data.

The court found Vantor had not identified any trade secret with enough specificity after two amended complaints. Vantor said it was "disappointed" and would review its options.
