# Meridale dismisses Southwest finance chief after internal review

**FICTIONAL. This article is invented for testing.**

*Phoenix Business Courier, April 28, 2026*

Meridale Lift Services has dismissed the regional controller of its Southwest region and two project managers in its Phoenix office after an internal audit review of subcontractor costs, according to three people familiar with the matter.

A Meridale spokesperson confirmed "personnel changes" and said the company "identified and corrected an accrual matter" and had strengthened its review procedures. The company would not say how much was involved.

A former project accountant in the Phoenix office, who asked not to be named, told the Courier that invoices from subcontractors on modernization jobs were "routinely held in a drawer" at quarter-ends, starting in 2024, so regional results would meet targets. Regional bonuses were tied to quarterly gross margin. The Courier could not confirm the account independently.

Meridale's Southwest region covers about 21,000 units in Arizona, Nevada and New Mexico.
