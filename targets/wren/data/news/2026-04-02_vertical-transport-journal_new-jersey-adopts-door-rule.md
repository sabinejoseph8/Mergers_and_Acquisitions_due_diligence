# New Jersey adopts door-safety retrofit rule

**FICTIONAL. This article is invented for testing.**

*Vertical Transport Journal, April 2, 2026*

New Jersey's Division of Codes and Standards adopted its door-lock monitoring and car door restrictor rule on April 1. Passenger elevators must comply by July 1, 2029, six months after the Illinois deadline.

The two states together have about 68,000 affected elevators, VTJ estimates. Independent service companies have the most exposure to older full-coverage contracts in both states. "Some of these contracts date to the early 2010s and renew every five years," one Newark-area consultant said. "The provider can't just reprice them."

Industry cost estimates remain US$11,000 to US$15,000 per car.
