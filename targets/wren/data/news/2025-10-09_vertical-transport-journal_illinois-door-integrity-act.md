# Illinois enacts Elevator Door Integrity Act; retrofit deadline set for end of 2028

**FICTIONAL. This article is invented for testing.**

*Vertical Transport Journal, October 9, 2025*

Illinois Governor signed the Elevator Door Integrity Act into law on Tuesday. It requires every passenger elevator in the state to have door-lock monitoring and car door restrictors by December 31, 2028. The law follows two fatal falls into hoistways in Chicago-area apartment buildings in 2024.

The state's Office of the Fire Marshal estimates that about 41,000 elevators will need work. Contractors interviewed by VTJ put the cost at **US$11,000 to US$15,000 per elevator**, depending on controller age and door equipment. Older relay-logic controllers may need full replacement.

Who pays depends on the service contract. "Owners with full-coverage contracts written before code-exclusion language became standard will expect their service provider to absorb it," said Raymond Kessel of the Building Owners Council of Illinois. "That fight is coming."

New Jersey's Division of Codes and Standards said it is drafting a similar rule.
