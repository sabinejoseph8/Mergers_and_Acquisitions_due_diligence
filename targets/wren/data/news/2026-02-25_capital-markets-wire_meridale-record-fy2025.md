# Meridale posts record 2025, guides to more growth

**FICTIONAL. This article is invented for testing.**

*Capital Markets Wire, February 25, 2026*

Meridale Lift Services (NYSE: MDLS) reported fiscal 2025 revenue of US$1.07 billion, up 8.5%, and organic growth of 5.4%, its best in four years. Adjusted EBITDA rose 5.3% to a record US$186.4 million. The company guided to fiscal 2026 Adjusted EBITDA of US$192 million to US$198 million.

Shares rose 6% to US$43.40.

On the call, analyst Joanna Ferris of Kettering Securities asked about a footnote showing the company now counts acquisitions as organic after six months rather than twelve. CFO Marisol Okafor said the change "reflects how fast we now integrate," and that on the old basis organic growth "would still have been healthy."

Okafor also said the company had lengthened the period over which it amortizes sales commissions to ten years, "in line with how long our customers actually stay."
