# State probes elevator service auto-renewals; building owners sue Meridale

**FICTIONAL. This article is invented for testing.**

*Garden State Ledger, July 8, 2026*

New Jersey's Division of Consumer Affairs is investigating how elevator service companies renew contracts and charge cancellation fees, according to a civil investigative demand reviewed by the Ledger. The demand, sent to Meridale Lift Services in March, asks for every renewal notice and cancellation fee charged since 2020.

Separately, Reyes Property Group, which owns 11 apartment buildings in Essex and Hudson counties, filed a proposed class action in May. It says Meridale renewed its contracts for five years without the notice required by the state's Service Contract Renewal Act, then charged fees of up to 50% of the remaining contract value when owners tried to leave.

The complaint estimates that Meridale collected about US$11.8 million in cancellation fees from New Jersey customers since 2020 and seeks triple that amount.

Meridale said its contracts "comply with applicable law" and that it would defend the case.
