# Ostrander Hale to take Meridale private for US$41.50 a share

**FICTIONAL. This article is invented for testing.**

*Capital Markets Wire, September 21, 2026*

Private equity firm Ostrander Hale Partners agreed to acquire Meridale Lift Services for US$41.50 per share in cash, valuing the elevator service company at about US$2.46 billion including debt. The price is 25% above Friday's close but below the US$44.10 the shares reached in February.

Chief Executive Dorian Pell will roll part of his stake and continue to lead the company. Ostrander Hale partner Simone Varga said the firm saw "a long runway in connected services and in modernizing the branch model."

The deal requires approval by a majority of unaffiliated holders. Meridale must also walk away from its pending US$96 million purchase of Brockmere Elevator. Closing is expected in the first quarter of 2027.
