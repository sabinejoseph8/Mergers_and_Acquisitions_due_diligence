# Lift Mechanics ratify four-year deal with 6.5% annual increases

**FICTIONAL. This article is invented for testing.**

*The Labor Standard, June 12, 2026*

Members of the International Brotherhood of Lift Mechanics ratified a new national agreement on June 11 by a 91% vote. The four-year contract, effective July 1, raises combined wages and benefits by 6.5% a year, the largest increase in the union's history. It also adds a ratio of one apprentice for every two mechanics on modernization jobs.

Negotiations went smoothly, both sides said, against a shortage of licensed mechanics that has pushed technician attrition to record highs. "Employers knew they had to pay to keep people," said union president Walt Brennan.

For service companies with price-capped contracts the cost is harder to pass on. An analyst at Kettering Securities estimated the new agreement adds about US$9 million of cost to Meridale Lift Services in the first year, growing each year.
